Subscribe to new entries

TAP publishes a feed of newly added entries. Add it to a feed reader and new TAP entries show up there alongside the other sites you follow, with no account or email signup needed on TAP.

The feed address

Copy this address:

https://tictacjoe.github.io/tap/feed.xml

How to subscribe

  1. Open a feed reader. That's an app or website that collects new posts from sites you follow. Free options include Feedly and Inoreader on the web, NetNewsWire on Mac and iPhone, and Feeder on Android.
  2. Look for a button labeled "Add feed", "Follow", "Subscribe" or "+".
  3. Paste the feed address above and confirm.

Many feed readers also accept the main site address, https://tictacjoe.github.io/tap/, and find the feed on their own.

Prefer email?

Feed-to-email services can send new feed items to your inbox. Search for "RSS to email" and give the service the feed address above.
