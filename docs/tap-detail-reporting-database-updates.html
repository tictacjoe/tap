About This Tracker

Reporting is a running archive of relevant news coverage, distinct in scale and purpose from the three curated trackers and from User Topics. Rather than researched and composed entry-by-entry, it's sourced almost entirely to established news organizations by design, and updated automatically as new coverage is published — giving a much broader, less curated view of daily developments than the other four databases.

Sweep Process

Keeping Reporting current is an automated data-ingestion pipeline rather than an editorial sweep: unlike the three curated trackers (Cabinet-Level Accountability, Corporate Deregulation, Government Service Redirection) or User Topics' own lighter-weight research, it's a pure scrape-and-parse pipeline that requires no AI reasoning or human editorial intervention to ingest and index new articles.

Stage 1: Single-Command Ingestion Pipeline

The entire ingestion sequence is orchestrated through an automated shell script. When executed, this pipeline performs three distinct automated operations:

  • Archive Scraping: Queries the specified archive endpoint to identify newly published posts that do not yet exist in the local dataset.
  • HTML Retrieval & Parsing: Fetches the raw HTML for each newly detected article, extracting metadata, publication timestamps, authors, itemized subsections, and primary external source URLs.
  • Dataset Compilation: Merges the parsed stories incrementally into the reporting datastore and rebuilds the fast lookup index.

Stage 2: Output Validation & Local Git Staging

Once the scraper completes, the pipeline outputs the exact count of newly ingested articles, verifies dataset integrity, and commits the records to the private research repository.

Stage 3: Two-Repository Publishing & Live Deployment

To make the newly ingested reporting searchable on the live public site:

  • Publication Engine: Compiles the updated reporting records into the public search feeds and refreshes homepage post counters.
  • Public Web Repository: Deploys the static search feeds and indices live to the public web portal.
