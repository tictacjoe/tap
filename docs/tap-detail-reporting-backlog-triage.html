Reporting: Backlog Triage

TAP — Docs

About This Tracker

Reporting is a running archive of relevant news coverage — see the companion "Reporting" doc for that database on its own. This doc covers a distinct process built on top of it: mining that archive for stories substantial enough to become full curated entries in one of the three structured, curated trackers:

  • Cabinet-Level Accountability
  • Corporate Deregulation
  • Government Service Redirection

Sweep Process

A Reporting backlog triage sweep is the hybrid data-mining and editorial curation workflow that discovers, clusters, researches, and promotes high-impact stories out of Reporting and into the three curated trackers above — bridging the gap between raw news volume and rigorous, source-verified database claims.

Stage 1: Automated Candidate Mining

The sweep initiates by running the candidate update script.

  • The classifier searches the raw reporting corpus for statutory violations, regulatory rollbacks, agency impoundments, and watchdog findings.
  • Extracted hits are appended to the candidates JSON file with a default status of "status": "untriaged".
  • Stable IDs prevent duplicate ingestion across overlapping scan ranges.

Stage 2: Semantic Clustering & Deduplication

When dealing with large candidate backlogs (100+ untriaged items), multiple news articles often cover the same ongoing real-world event. To eliminate redundant research passes:

  • Threshold Sweep: Evaluates cluster size distributions across similarity thresholds.
  • Apply Clustering: Groups stories by event, stamping each record with a cluster_id and designating one is_representative candidate per cluster.

Stage 3: Batch Export & AI Research Pre-Pass

For each representative candidate cluster:

  • Batch Slicing: Export candidate batches using the batching script.
  • AI Pre-Pass Research: Execute the research pre-pass prompt to independently query primary legal dockets, Federal Register filings, and agency inspector general reports.
  • URL & Citation Verification: Run the verification script to validate that all referenced URLs and citations are live, accessible, and factually corroborating.

Stage 4: Triage Decision & Promotion

Each candidate is evaluated against strict project inclusion criteria:

  • Promotion to Curated Trackers: If a candidate meets the evidentiary threshold (a named official, a specific statutory/regulatory violation, and 2+ independent sources), a new JSON entry is created in the target tracker and stamped with date_added.
  • Declining Candidates: If the candidate lacks direct agency action or constitutes general political commentary, it is marked "status": "declined" in the candidates dataset with an explicit reason recorded.
  • Audit Logging: All decisions and rationale are appended to the triage history file.

Stage 5: Two-Repository Publishing & Live Deployment

Once triage is complete:

  • Private Research Repository: Staged, committed with batch triage metrics, and pushed.
  • Publication Engine: The compiler regenerates all live JSON feeds and updates candidate counters.
  • Public Web Repository: Changes are committed and pushed, deploying promoted updates to the web portal.
