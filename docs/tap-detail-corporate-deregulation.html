About This Tracker

Corporate Deregulation tracks federal agency rule rollbacks, compliance stays, and statutory rollbacks across environmental, labor, consumer safety, and financial domains — actions by agencies including the EPA, OSHA, DOL, DOE, CFPB, FCC, DOT, MSHA, and USDA that reduce or remove regulatory oversight of corporate or industry activity.

Of the three curated trackers, Corporate Deregulation draws most heavily on direct government sources — agency filings and official statements — supplemented by investigative and mainstream reporting. Each entry keeps the verifiable regulatory action itself, as shown on the Federal Register, distinct from any modeled economic or health impact, which must be explicitly attributed to a named source such as the agency's own cost-benefit analysis or an academic policy tracker rather than presented as established fact.

Sweep Process

A Corporate Deregulation sweep is the structured audit and verification workflow that keeps every entry current: detecting entries that may be stale or still pending, researching what has actually changed, and republishing corrected records to the live site.

Stage 1: Rule-Lifecycle & Staleness Detection

The sweep begins by running the deregulation staleness scanner.

The script audits every JSON entry in the datastore focusing on the regulatory lifecycle:

  • Non-Finalized Rule Statuses: It specifically flags rules whose status contains "Proposed" or "Delayed/Paused", because pending rules are subject to public comment periods, congressional review, or judicial stays.
  • Verification Age Check: Flags entries where last_verified is older than 60 days.

Stage 2: Federal Register, Regulatory Docket & Impact Research

For all flagged entries (processed in focused batches of approximately 6):

  • Federal Register & Court Docket Searches: Targeted searches check whether proposed rules were finalized, comment periods closed, compliance dates arrived, or federal courts issued stays under the Administrative Procedure Act (APA).
  • Resolving Internal Flags: If an entry contains a flagged follow-up question (for example, whether a passing compliance deadline altered enforcement under the MSHA silica rule), the sweep resolves that question directly in the primary text.
  • Attributed Impact Verification: Verifies that figures in estimated_health_impact (e.g. premature mortalities or financial harm) are explicitly cited to named models (e.g., NYU Policy Integrity tracker, agency Regulatory Impact Analyses).
  • Per-Batch Research Notes: If an entry undergoes a Real Correction (e.g., a rule previously described as active was stayed by a court), the search trail and legal analysis are written to a research brief file.

Stage 3: Schema Conformance & Named Accountability Enforcement

The updates are written programmatically, verifying strict adherence to the schema file:

  • primary_proponent: Requires a specific named individual (the agency head or deputy driving the rollback) rather than a generic administration label.
  • corporate_beneficiaries: Validates that listed corporate or trade association beneficiaries are tied to on-the-record lobbying filings or public support.
  • short_summary: Hand-crafted, 80–110 character sentence leading directly with the concrete action ("Repealed X," "Removed the requirement that...").
  • Timestamp Integrity: Bumps last_verified to the current date while preserving date_added (the fixed historical record of when the entry entered the database).
  • section_summaries Completeness Gate: Re-runs the validation script to confirm every entry's section_summaries object has non-empty what_changed, estimated_impact, and confidence_note values before publishing.

Stage 4: Two-Repository Publishing & Live Deployment

The sweep completes with atomic synchronization and static compilation:

  • Private Research Repository: Staged, validated, committed with batch summaries, and pushed.
  • Publication Engine: Compiles the raw JSON files into the public deregulation dataset, updates domain category counts (environmental, labor, consumer_safety, financial), and refreshes client-side search indices.
  • Public Web Repository: Committed and pushed, instantly deploying the updated deregulation tracker to the web portal.
