About This Tracker

User Topics is a lighter-weight fact-checking layer for topics submitted directly by readers through the site's Suggest a Topic feature. While the primary curated trackers focus strictly on named individual statutory liability, formal agency rule rollbacks, and institutional degradation, users may want to surface emerging policy questions, viral social media claims, and local impacts that merit rigorous, independent examination — and rather than discarding suggestions that fall outside those narrow parameters, this tracker investigates each real topic, stamps it with an objective verdict (confirmed, partially_confirmed, unable_to_verify, or misleading_as_stated), and contextualizes it with verified primary sources. A submission that clears a curated tracker's own evidentiary bar gets promoted there instead, with a pointer left here so the original question stays publicly traceable.

Sweep Process

A User Topics and Submission sweep is the interactive intake and verification workflow that turns a raw reader submission into a published, source-checked entry -- or a promotion into one of the curated trackers -- and publishes a transparent audit record on the public site.

Stage 1: Public Intake & Queue Auditing

The intake lifecycle begins when a reader submits a topic through the in-page submission modal on the live site.

Submissions are captured in a structured intake sheet, recording the submission timestamp, the raw claim description, and an optional source link. During a triage sweep:

  • Queue Ingestion: Untriaged submissions (rows with an empty status) are retrieved and sorted chronologically.
  • Batch Partitioning: To maintain deep factual rigor and prevent superficial skimming, submissions are audited in focused batches of approximately 6 items.
  • Scope Verification: Each item is checked to confirm it pertains to federal government activity, agency directives, or public policy.

Stage 2: Targeted Primary Research & Fit Classification

For every submission in the batch, researchers execute 1 to 3 targeted web searches across official agency releases, court records, Federal Register notices, and established investigative reporting. Each submission is classified into one of three distinct outcomes:

  • Direct Promotion to Curated Tracker: If a submission clears the strict evidentiary standard (a named official, a specific statutory or regulatory violation, and 2+ independent primary sources), it is converted into a full curated entry. A lightweight pointer entry is created in User Topics to ensure the submitter's query remains publicly traceable.
  • User Topics Fact-Check: If the topic is authentic and verifiable but represents a broad policy trend, an agency-wide shift without single-official liability, or a disputed factual claim, it is authored as an entry in the fifth datastore. Every entry receives an objective verdict (confirmed, partially_confirmed, unable_to_verify, or misleading_as_stated), a 2-4 sentence plain-language summary, and corroborating citations.
  • Off-Site Decline: Submissions representing commercial spam, non-governmental commentary, or exact duplicates are marked as declined directly in the intake queue with an explicit reason recorded.

Stage 3: Schema Validation & Traceability Enforcement

All verified User Topics entries are authored programmatically, enforcing strict schema compliance:

  • Preserving Raw Submissions: The submitter's exact wording is preserved verbatim in the submitted claim field, ensuring transparency while presenting a cleaned topic headline for site navigation.
  • Cross-Tracker Promotion Anchors: If an item was promoted to a curated tracker, a cross-reference tag creates an interactive link connecting the community card directly to the detailed tracker record.
  • Schema Validation Gate: Re-runs programmatic validation scripts to verify that all required fields, verdict enums, source URLs, and date formats conform to project specifications before building.

Stage 4: Two-Repository Publishing & Live Deployment

The sweep concludes by synchronizing the local research datastore and deploying the static public feed:

  • Private Research Repository: Validated records are staged, committed with batch triage metrics, and pushed.
  • Publication Engine: Compiles the raw JSON files into the public community topics dataset, recalculates topic statistics, and generates interactive claim cards.
  • Public Web Repository: Changes are committed and pushed, instantly publishing the verified fact-checks and promoted topics to the web portal.
